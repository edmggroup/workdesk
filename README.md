# Faculty Workdesk

A private web app for the day-to-day work of a university professor: the semester, teaching, exams, mentoring, research, projects and administration in one place, with a dashboard and automatic reminders. It is built in the same style as the Research Group Logbook: one HTML page, a Google Sheet as the database, and installable on phones.

## What it covers

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Academic calendar** | Semesters (start day, end day, optional last teaching day), academic-schedule entries on specific dates — CIA-I, CIA-II, practical exams, department / school invigilation (with times), viva, exams, fests, deadlines — the next CIA / exam shows on the dashboard, and holidays. Holidays can be pasted in bulk. A schedule entry can suspend regular classes, or make a day follow another day's timetable |
| **Timetable** | Theory and lab slots, with batches for parallel labs. Works with **weekday** or **day-order** timetables (day order skips holidays automatically). Includes a weekly grid, a *This week* view with real dates, and clash detection |
| **Question banks & papers** | *Examinations → Question Banks & Papers*: question bank entries, question paper setting, answer keys / scheme of evaluation, scrutiny / moderation, lab and viva questions — with the course, exam (CIA-I, CIA-II, end-semester…), units, number of questions required and done (progress bar), pattern / blueprint, CO / Bloom's mapping, who asked, received and due date / time, how to submit (portal, email, sealed cover…) and the link, status (through *Returned for revision*) and the documents. Due dates are on the calendar and reminded 5 days ahead until submitted |
| **CIA repeats & exceptions** | *Examinations → CIA repeats & exceptions*: repeat CIAs, make-up tests, repeat practicals, improvement tests, assignments in lieu of CIA, special exams and other exceptions allotted to you, with who allotted them, the course, the date and time assured to the students, venue, maximum marks and the marks deadline. Each has its students (picked from your class lists or pasted from Excel) with the reason, status and marks. Shown in the calendar and dashboard, reminded in alerts and the morning email (fix a date, the exam date, submit marks), checked for clashes, listed in reports, and shown in each student's history |
| **Make-up classes** | A class marked *Not held*, or adjusted as skipped / to be rescheduled, is reminded as *Make-up class owed* until a make-up is scheduled (choose *Make-up needed: No* to stop it). One tap opens a make-up extra class already filled in; upcoming make-up classes are alerted ahead |
| **Clashes & class adjustments** | Every form with a date (events, meetings, exam duties, academic schedule, leave, conferences, extra classes, mentoring, actions, reminders) shows what else is on that date — your classes, CIA / exams, holidays, events, meetings and duties — and marks overlaps in red, with an *Adjustments / arrangements* note. When something overlaps one of your classes you are reminded (bell, dashboard, morning email) until you record a **class adjustment**: substituted, swapped, rescheduled to a make-up class (added to that day's timetable), combined, conducted online, assignment given, or skipped — not held. Adjusted classes show on the dashboard and timetable and are not counted as unlogged; reports list them |
| **Extra classes** | Extra, additional, remedial, make-up, substitution and bridge classes, added to the timetable for that date only |
| **Work diary** | Log each class: unit, topic, method, attendance and hours. The app knows which classes up to yesterday are **still unlogged** (today's are not counted until the day is over). *Continue from last class* fills in the topic for you, and you can mark a class *Not held*. A printable diary with HoD/Dean signature lines, plus Excel export |
| **Courses** | Unit-wise planned hours, with coverage worked out from the diary, plus average attendance and weekly load. Each course links to its syllabus document, Google Classroom / Moodle and course page — shown on the course card and next to today's classes on the dashboard |
| **Exams** | Exam duties (invigilation, squad, QP setting, practical examiner…). Evaluations with script counts, a progress bar, and *scripts per day needed* to finish on time |
| **Class teacher** | Your class(es) with students (paste from Excel), attendance % with automatic flags below the requirement (75% by default), a follow-up list, and a log of class meetings, attendance reviews, parent communication and student issues, each with a follow-up date that is reminded |
| **Weekly review** | A page per week: classes taken (by course, with topics), meetings, events, actions and reminders completed, exam work, student follow-ups and research, plus everything coming next week. *Copy as text* for WhatsApp / email, or print it |
| **Leave & OD** | Casual, duty / OD, earned, medical and other leave with yearly quotas and balances. Outside-campus events without an OD record are flagged (*Record OD* fills the form). Warns when classes fall during a leave so a substitution can be arranged |
| **Marks** | CIA / internal assessments per course: quick keyboard entry (Enter moves to the next student), average / highest / lowest, students below the pass mark highlighted and optionally added to the class follow-up list, consolidated and weighted totals, Excel export |
| **Mentoring** | Mentees (bulk paste from Excel), individual and group meetings, notes, agreed actions and follow-up dates. Flags mentees you haven't met in N days. A mentee can be picked from your class students; each student then shows class follow-ups, mentoring meetings, marks and attendance together |
| **Research** | Plans with milestones; a publications pipeline (fill a publication from its DOI) from Idea to Published, with indexing and quartile; collaborations; conferences and workshops with abstract and registration deadlines; peer review and editorial roles; a researcher database (your IDs, an h-index and citation history with a trend line, and contacts) |
| **Conferences & workshops** | Shown as boxes: each conference has four date tiles side by side — abstract submission, full paper, registration & fee, and the event from–to — each marked *in N days*, *✓ done* or *passed*, plus the registration fee and whether it is paid, your paper / talk title, the event website link and documents, and an overlap badge. Upcoming / Past / All, and a Table view. Abstract, full-paper and registration / fee deadlines are reminded until done |
| **Events** | One-off or **recurring** events (weekly … yearly) with a session log to record minutes/notes each time. *On campus / Outside campus / Online* — outside-campus events are always alerted at least 3 days ahead. One page for all events, in 7 categories: FDP / training programmes, institutional events, events you are in charge of, research events, external conferences & workshops, teaching-related events and other academic activities. Each has dates, your role, a registration deadline and a task checklist. Conferences from the Research section appear here too |
| **Events you organise** | *Events → Organised by me* lists the events where you are in-charge / coordinator or organiser separately, grouped as Being planned, Happening now, Recurring series, Over — report pending, and Completed. Each card shows the dates and venue, approval, planning-checklist progress with the next and overdue tasks, participants expected and attended, budget requested / sanctioned / spent, chief guest / resource persons, team, and report status. The event form shows these planning fields only for your own events, and *+ Standard planning checklist* adds the usual tasks (approval, budget, venue, brochure, guests, registration, certificates, report to IQAC, accounts, NAAC upload) dated from the event. Approvals still pending two weeks before, and reports not submitted afterwards, are reminded. In both event lists, an event that overlaps other work (your classes, meetings, duties, leave, other events) shows a small red badge with the number of overlaps; tap it to open the details under that row, with *Adjust* buttons for clashing classes |
| **Researcher database** | Each researcher has designation, department, institution, city / country, a **category** (Academic, Premier institution — IIT / IISc / IISER / NIT, National lab / R&D, International, Industry, Funding agency), expertise, facilities they can offer, relation (incl. Co-PI, mentor, agency officer), how you met, profile / ORCID, h-index, and the **proposals / projects and funding calls** they are connected to. The list is filtered by category. Projects and calls show their connected researchers, with *Link* and *+ New researcher* |
| **Emails & follow-up log** | Researchers, projects, funding calls and collaborations keep a log of emails sent / received, calls, meetings and messages — date, subject, link to the email thread, and a follow-up date. Follow-ups are reminded (bell, dashboard, morning email) until ticked *Done* |
| **Projects & grants** | Three stages: **Going to apply**, **Applied** and **Sanctioned** (plus *Not funded*). Each project keeps the concept note, objectives, proposal and sanction-order documents, PI, Co-PIs and collaborators, funding agency and scheme, budget requested and sanctioned, reference number, planned submission date, milestones, progress log, report / UC dates, and a timeline |
| **Funding calls** | Track funding opportunities with the announcement date, application opening, your planned submission date, hard-copy date, last date and submission date. Each date is reminded, a countdown shows the days to the last date, and *Start proposal* creates a linked project |
| **Achievements & certificates** | Course completions (NPTEL / SWAYAM / Coursera / MOOCs), FDP and workshop certificates, professional certifications, awards, best-paper awards, recognitions, memberships, patents and invited talks, with issuer, level, grade, certificate number and the certificate link or Drive file. Courses in progress are reminded (assignment deadline, exam date, course end), as are certificates still to download and renewals. *To do* lists certificates not yet on file. *+ Certificate* on a finished event or conference fills the form. They also appear in the appraisal report |
| **Administration** | Responsibilities and in-charges, committees, meetings and minutes (create action items straight from a meeting), action items and reminders with **Pending / Overdue / Completed / Archived** tabs. Nothing disappears when done or past its date; completion dates and repeat history are kept |
| **Connections** | Events, meetings, action items and reminders can be linked to the committee, responsibility, class (class teacher), unit or office — department, school, university, HED, research and other centres, examination office — event, project or course they belong to. The standard units and offices are set up for you under *Administration → Units & offices*; rename them or add more. Each of those records shows everything connected to it, with *+ Add* buttons; *Backup & restore → Check connections* finds links to deleted records |
| **Notepad** | Jot notes on the dashboard (or the Notepad page). *File into…* turns a note into an action item, reminder, meeting, diary entry, exam duty, publication and more, with the form already filled in: the first line becomes the title, and any date or time in the note ("Friday 3pm", "by 20 Nov") is picked up. Filed notes keep a link to where they went |
| **Dashboard** | Today's classes with one-click logging; items that need attention; the semester week and teaching days left; KPI tiles; the next 14 days; syllabus coverage; research pipeline; project progress against time elapsed |
| **Reminders** | Per category, on/off and how many days ahead. They show in the bell and on the dashboard, and arrive as a **morning email digest**, with a week preview on Mondays. The app and the email use the same code, so they always agree |
| **Reports** | Built like the logbook's Reports page. *Annual self-appraisal* (Teaching / Research / Administration / Extension), *Activity report* for any period (activity summary, a teaching summary per course, then every section), *By course* (syllabus coverage, diary, extra classes, evaluations) and *By section*. Print or save as PDF, or download as text, CSV or Excel. Each report ends with "End of Report — system-generated, no signature required" |
| **Documents & links** | Attach Google Drive or OneDrive share links to responsibilities, committees, meetings, action items, projects, evaluations, exam duties and other records. Responsibilities also have a paperwork checklist with due dates, which feeds into your reminders |
| **Overlap badges everywhere** | Meetings (both tabs), exam duties, academic schedule, leave, conferences, mentoring meetings, extra classes and events — and every item on the dashboard and in alerts — show a small red badge with the number of things they overlap (your classes, meetings, duties, events, leave). Tap it to open the detail under that row: tap a line to open it, *Adjust* a clashing class, *Won’t attend* the other meeting / event (that date only for repeating ones, with Undo), or *Note arrangement*. Deadlines and reminders are not counted as overlaps |
| **Attendance** | Meetings: *Attended* (you took part), *Held* (you convened it), *Did not attend*, *Postponed*, plus your role (member, convener / chair, secretary, invitee, presenter). Events: *Attended* and *Did not attend* alongside Completed / Not attending. Repeating meetings and events record attendance per date. An **Attended** button on today's and past meetings and events (dashboard, alerts) marks it in one tap. Minutes are only asked for meetings you convened or took minutes of, or that are still marked Scheduled |
| **Minutes & records** | Meetings and events can repeat; each date keeps its own minutes / session record. Meetings without minutes, missing session records and in-charge events without a report are reminded until they are recorded |
| **Import from Excel** | Any section: paste rows from Excel / Google Sheets or choose an .xlsx / .csv file; columns are matched by name, dates in any common format |
| **Safety** | Undo after delete; auto-lock with PIN after a chosen idle time; a change history of every add, edit and delete (deleted records can be restored from it); files uploaded straight to a Drive folder from any *Documents & links* field |
| **Data** | JSON backup and restore, instant open from the copy kept on the device, opening any backup file read-only, everything exported to Excel, calendar export (.ics) for Google Calendar or Outlook, nightly Drive backups, and sample data you can remove in one click |

## Files

```
index.html        the whole app (UI + logic)
backend/Code.gs   Google Apps Script backend (database, PIN check, digest email, backups)
manifest.json, sw.js, icons/   make it installable on phones
SETUP.md          step-by-step deployment
```

See **SETUP.md** to deploy it. Until it is connected, the app runs in **local mode** (stored in the browser), so you can try it straight away.
